Wick 0.2.0 adds an app uninstaller, explains the space Windows doesn't show you, uses graphics cards better, and
fixes a long list of freezes and rough edges. Updating keeps your settings and History.

## New

- **Uninstall apps you no longer use.** Clean Up › Apps lists installed apps with their size and when you last
  opened them, with the ones you haven't opened in six months at the top. Uninstall runs the app's own
  uninstaller, then moves the folders it left behind to the Recycle Bin, so you can still get them back.
- **App Leftovers** finds folders in AppData and ProgramData that belong to apps you removed long ago. Nothing is
  selected for you, since Wick can't always be sure a folder is unused.
- **What "Everything else" is.** On Home, Explain shows the space outside your folders: the hibernation file,
  page file, restore points and WSL or Docker disks on Windows, and system logs, package caches, Flatpak and
  Snap on Linux, each with a safe way to free it.
- **A heads-up when a drive is almost full.** Wick can let you know, or also clear the app caches it marks as
  safe (Settings › When a drive is almost full).
- **Smaller subtitle fonts:** MKV videos with styled subtitles keep only the characters the subtitles use from
  their fonts, and fonts nothing uses are left out. On AV1 and HEVC videos this happens without re-encoding.
- **Fonts in documents:** Word, PowerPoint and PDF files keep only the parts of their embedded fonts that the
  document uses. PDFs are drawn before and after to make sure every page looks exactly the same.
- **E-books and comic books** (.epub, .cbz): pictures stored smaller with identical pixels, and e-book fonts cut
  to the characters in the book.
- **Motion Photos** from Pixel phones are no longer skipped: the photo is made smaller with identical pixels and
  the clip re-encoded.
- **RAW + JPEG** and **Phone Backups** in Clean Up: photos saved twice by the camera, and old iPhone, iPad or
  Samsung backups, never selected for you. Compacting now also covers Hyper-V, VirtualBox and VMware disks.
- **Live Photos** are no longer skipped: the photo is made smaller losslessly and the clip keeps its link to it.
- **SVG drawings** are cleaned up and checked to look the same; **ZIP files** can have their pictures shrunk.
- **Camera Proxies** in Clean Up finds the low-resolution copies GoPro, DJI and Insta360 cameras save next to
  every video, and the Windows tool now finds installer packages no installed program needs.
- **More inside documents:** unused pictures, page thumbnails and repeated pictures or fonts in PDFs are
  removed, BMP pictures in Office files become PNG, and black-and-white scans in PDFs are stored the way fax
  machines do (often a third smaller). Options leave out cropped-away parts of pictures, Excel's pivot caches
  and Illustrator's editing data.
- **Podcasts and audiobooks** are recognised and stored as mono at a lower bitrate when audio is re-encoded.
- **Video soundtracks:** uncompressed audio in MKVs is packed as FLAC (same samples), and two new options leave
  out lower-quality copies of a Blu-ray soundtrack or mix surround down to stereo.
- **Hide banding:** a Film grain choice that also adds a light grain to clean AV1 videos, which breaks up the
  visible steps in skies and dark scenes.
- **Ctrl+K** opens a command bar: type to jump to any page or Clean Up tool, or run an action.
- **Smaller files or faster?** Wick asks once, suggests an answer for your computer, and sets JPEG XL effort and
  a new processor speed option for videos. Both can be changed later in the compression options.
- **Stop any task** from the running-tasks panel in the sidebar.
- **Scoop:** `scoop bucket add wick https://github.com/Lanntern/Wick`, then `scoop install wick`.
- **AI assistants** can now look through Clean Up's findings too (read-only).

## Better

- **Graphics cards:** NVIDIA encodes use a full-resolution first pass, temporal AQ and B-frame references; Intel
  uses look-ahead; AMD switches from a fixed QP to quality-based rate control with pre-analysis. Each is only
  used if your card and driver accept it.
- **Duplicates are found several times faster**, hashing files in parallel with BLAKE3. Hard drives are read
  one file at a time instead, which is faster for them.
- **FLAC** files come out about 1% smaller.
- **Video audio:** the audio bitrate now scales with the channels (5.1 gets twice as much as stereo), and tracks
  already at or under it are copied instead of re-encoded.
- **Album art and video covers** are stored smaller with the same pixels, every picture of an album included. Songs with big album art get just
  that, with the sound untouched.
- **Hidden previews and thumbnails** inside camera photos are left out, and colour profiles that are sRGB in
  disguise become a compact one.
- **Video soundtracks** that are encoded: identical stereo becomes mono, talk gets a lower bitrate, and silent
  ones can be left out. Screen recordings are encoded with tools made for them, and TV recordings are rewrapped
  without their padding.
- **iPhone HDR videos** (Dolby Vision) can be re-encoded when they need to be smaller: the HDR picture stays.
- **Upsampled "hi-res" music** (CD audio saved at 96 kHz or more) goes back to its real rate, and big empty
  spaces in FLAC and MP3 tags are removed.
- **Files Wick made** (videos, audio, JPEGs and PNGs) carry a short mark, so they aren't compressed again, even
  after being moved.
- **Safer previews and scans:** thumbnails, previews, Broken Files and Similar Photos decode files in a separate
  process with fewer rights, so a damaged or malicious file can't take Wick down with it.
- **Quitting never freezes**, even with work running, and nothing is left half-done.
- **Faster start-up**, and the first thumbnail no longer waits.
- **Every dialog** now has the same layout and button order, and settings and tooltips say less, more plainly.
- **Tooltips** appear as a card under the control instead of following the pointer.
- **Undo** is offered right after a job, and file types you've turned off can be turned on from the result.
- **Compress Apps** shows sizes, estimates and per-folder settings more clearly, with optional community results
  from CompactGUI.
- **Bundled tools updated:** libjxl 0.13.0, gifsicle 1.97 and Pandoc 3.14.

## Fixed

- Some videos were rejected as "Output video has decoding errors" when FFmpeg only printed a harmless message
  that the original also produced.
- Ultra HDR photos (recent Pixel and Samsung phones) lost their HDR when compressed, and other pictures stored
  after a photo were dropped. iPhone and Samsung HDR photos in HEIC, portrait depth maps and spatial photos are
  now left alone instead of losing those parts.
- Many ordinary Samsung photos were skipped as Motion Photos; their own data after the photo is now kept.
- An FFmpeg that printed an error and then stopped responding could make a job wait forever; it's stopped now.
- MP4 videos lost their cover picture when re-encoded.
- TV recordings with teletext couldn't be saved as MKV.
- Videos with 5.1 audio using side speakers (common in MKVs from Blu-rays) failed when an audio bitrate was set.
- Large Metadata scans could make the window stop responding.
- The running-tasks button didn't shorten when the sidebar got narrow.

## When something goes wrong

- If Wick closes unexpectedly, it says so at the next start and offers to report it, with the details attached.
- Settings › Report a Problem copies the diagnostics and opens the bug report form.
- The log now records what failed and why, instead of every routine step.
